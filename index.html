What we do

A property company built around its own portfolio

Our work covers the full life of a property: buying it, letting it, running it and, when the time is right, selling it.

01

Property acquisition

We buy real estate for our own portfolio, looking at each property’s condition, location and long-term potential before we commit.

02

Lettings

We let properties that we own or lease, providing well-kept spaces to rent for tenants.

03

Property operation

We operate and look after the properties we let, keeping them in good order for the people who live or work in them.

04

Property sales

We sell properties from our own portfolio when it makes sense to do so, dealing with buyers in a clear and straightforward way.

About us

A new property company with a clear focus

JS Property & Estates Private Ltd was incorporated in England in November 2025 and is run by director Jagir Singh.

The company concentrates on buying, selling and letting its own real estate, and on operating the properties it owns or leases. Its registered office is in Acocks Green, Birmingham.
